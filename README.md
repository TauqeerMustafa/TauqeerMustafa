## About

I'm a BS Cybersecurity student at Air University Islamabad and founder of **Tauqeer Mustafa Inc. (TMI)**, a cybersecurity and technology consultancy based in Islamabad, Pakistan. TMI works with SMEs on security assessments, incident response, compliance, and web development.

My interests sit at the intersection of offensive security and applied AI in cybersecurity tooling. I hold Google and Microsoft cybersecurity certifications and I've co-authored two peer-reviewed publications.
