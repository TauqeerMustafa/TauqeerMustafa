## Profile

I'm a **BS Cybersecurity** student at Air University Islamabad and founder of **Tauqeer Mustafa Inc. (TMI)** — a cybersecurity and technology consultancy in Islamabad, Pakistan, built to bring enterprise-grade security practices to small and mid-sized businesses that are usually priced out of them.

TMI's practice covers:

```
├── Security Assessments    → vulnerability scanning, risk review, hardening
├── Incident Response       → investigation, containment, remediation
├── Compliance Advisory     → aligning SME process with recognized frameworks
└── Web Development         → secure, production-grade client infrastructure
```

Outside of client work, I build small, dependency-light developer and security tooling — CLIs that solve one problem well rather than frameworks that solve none of them completely. My personal interest sits at the intersection of **offensive security** and **applied AI** in security workflows.

I hold cybersecurity certifications from **Google** and **Microsoft**, and I've co-authored two peer-reviewed papers, in *Clinical Infectious Diseases* (2023) and *Expert Review of Anti-infective Therapy* (2023).
